Refund & Cancellation Policy

How cancellation and refund requests are handled for payments made directly to Vishnu EV.

Effective date: 30 October 2026

1. Scope

This policy applies only to payments made directly to Vishnu EV for a product or service where Vishnu EV is the named seller or service provider. If you paid a third-party charging network, property owner, equipment supplier or payment platform, that provider's refund and cancellation terms may apply instead.

2. Charging sessions

For a charging session, please check the displayed tariff and applicable session details before starting. If you believe you were charged incorrectly, contact us promptly with the transaction reference, station location, date and time, amount paid and a brief description. We will review the available transaction and charger records and communicate the outcome. Refunds for verified billing errors will be handled in accordance with applicable law and the payment provider's process.

3. Advance bookings and reservations

If a station or service offers advance booking, the cancellation, rescheduling and refund conditions applicable to that booking will be shown at the time of booking or in the relevant written confirmation. Do not assume a booking is refundable unless the applicable booking terms say so or a refund is required by law.

4. Equipment, installation and project orders

Orders for chargers, electrical equipment, installation, civil work or other project services may involve procurement, mobilisation, engineering and third-party costs. Cancellation requests must be made in writing. Any amount refundable will be assessed against work completed, goods procured, non-recoverable costs and the written quotation or agreement, subject always to applicable law.

5. Deposits, advances and security amounts

The treatment of any deposit, advance or security amount will be governed by the specific quotation, invoice or signed agreement under which it was paid. This policy does not create a blanket right to retain or refund a deposit; the applicable written terms and law will govern.

6. How to request a refund or cancellation

Contact us at Support@Vishnuevcharging.com or WhatsApp +91 97588 96217. Include your full name, contact number, payment or invoice reference, date, amount, station or project details, and the reason for your request. We may ask for supporting documents to verify the transaction.

7. Review and processing

We will acknowledge your request and review it within a reasonable time. If approved, refunds will generally be sent to the original payment method where practicable. The time taken for the funds to appear depends on banks, payment gateways and other payment providers. Any applicable statutory rights remain unaffected.

8. Exceptions and legal rights

Nothing in this policy limits consumer rights or remedies that cannot be excluded under applicable Indian law. If there is a conflict between this policy and a signed agreement or mandatory law, the applicable agreement or law will prevail to the extent of the conflict.

9. Changes to this policy

We may revise this policy by publishing an updated version on this page.

10. Contact

For refund or cancellation support, contact Support@Vishnuevcharging.com or WhatsApp +91 97588 96217.

Important: This is a conservative draft because Vishnu EV's exact consumer charging, booking, equipment-sale and deposit terms have not been fully specified. Confirm the actual refund rules for each product/service and obtain legal review before publishing. Do not advertise a fixed refund window or “non-refundable” rule unless it matches your contracts and applicable law.
