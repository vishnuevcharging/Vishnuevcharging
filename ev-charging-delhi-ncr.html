Local project planning

Plan the right charging site for Delhi NCR.

Delhi NCR has a mix of logistics activity, commercial destinations and fleet operations. A charging project should be evaluated against the vehicles using the site, daily energy demand, parking and dwell time, electrical connection and access for larger vehicles.

  • Assess vehicle types, fleet size and charging schedules
  • Review site access, layout and available space
  • Evaluate electrical load and connection requirements
  • Discuss charger capacity, project costs and operating model
