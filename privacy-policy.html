Privacy Policy

How Vishnu EV collects, uses and protects information shared through our website and business enquiries.

Effective date: 30 October 2026

1. About this policy

This Privacy Policy explains how Vishnu EV (“Vishnu EV”, “we”, “us” or “our”) handles personal information when you visit our website, contact us, submit an enquiry, discuss a charging project, or otherwise interact with our business.

2. Information we may collect

Depending on how you interact with us, we may receive your name, phone number, email address, city or project location, business or property details, vehicle/fleet requirements, and the contents of messages or enquiries you send us. Basic technical information such as browser, device, log and usage data may also be collected by the website host or service providers.

3. How we use information

  • Respond to enquiries and provide customer support.
  • Assess potential charging locations, fleet requirements and partnership proposals.
  • Prepare quotations, proposals and project communications when requested.
  • Operate, secure, maintain and improve our website and services.
  • Meet legal, regulatory, accounting and security obligations.
  • Send business updates or marketing communications where permitted. You may ask us to stop marketing messages.

4. WhatsApp and other communication channels

If you choose to contact us through WhatsApp, your message and the information you choose to include will be processed by WhatsApp and delivered to our business account. Please avoid sending sensitive personal or financial information through public or unsecured channels.

5. Sharing of information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share relevant information with service providers who support website hosting, communications, customer management or business operations, and with professional advisers or authorities where required by law or necessary to protect our rights. We seek to limit such sharing to what is reasonably necessary.

6. Retention and security

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described in this policy, including enquiry follow-up, project records, legal compliance and dispute resolution. We use reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no internet transmission or storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

7. Your choices and requests

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information you have provided, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements. You may also withdraw consent where processing is based on consent.

8. Third-party services and links

Our website may link to third-party services, including WhatsApp, maps, payment or charging-network platforms. Those services operate under their own privacy policies and terms. We are not responsible for their independent practices.

9. Children

Our website and business services are intended for business and general audiences and are not directed to children. We do not knowingly seek personal information from children.

10. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The revised version will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions or requests, contact us at Support@Vishnuevcharging.com or WhatsApp +91 97588 96217.
